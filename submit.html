Submit an Eggy Cup

process a cup from your game log
Played the cup? Your game log has every round. Drop it below, check that the result looks right, and send it. From the end of the cup until Sunday midday (Spain time) it gets processed within about 15 minutes by an automatic job, then published once aizpun has checked it. A log sent later waits until the next cup night.
  • The log is C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Zeepkist\BepInEx\LogOutput.log
  • The game overwrites it every time it starts, so grab it before relaunching Zeepkist.
  • Your file is read here in your browser first. Nothing is uploaded until you press Submit.
  • Roulette nights and other special events are added by hand. Just send the log to aizpun.
